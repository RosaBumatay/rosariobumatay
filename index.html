AI Customer Support Ticket Routing & Response Automation

Problem
Support requests pile up in one central inbox. Someone had to manually inspect each inquiry, rate urgency, and alert the on-call agent.
What I did
Engineered a multi-step Zapier workflow. Form submissions sync to Google Sheets, receive AI category and sentiment classification via ChatGPT, dispatch automated Gmail confirmations, and broadcast priority-tiered alerts to dedicated Slack channels.
Result
Immediate ticket logging, zero-delay sentiment triaging, and real-time triage visibility in team Slack with no human bottlenecks.

Production architecture demo project.

n8n • GPT-4 • Webhooks • CRM Sync

AI Lead Qualification & Pipeline Routing System

Problem
Incoming leads lingered in spreadsheet queues awaiting manual review, lowering conversion velocity for high-intent prospects.
What I did
Built an n8n webhook workflow that triggers on row addition. GPT evaluates ICP fit, scoring leads into Hot, Warm, or Cold. Hot prospects instantly trigger an urgent sales Slack ping, while Warm leads receive targeted drip follow-ups via Gmail.
Result
Immediate automated lead scoring and tailored response dispatch without delay, dramatically accelerating speed-to-lead.

Production architecture demo project.
